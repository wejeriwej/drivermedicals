  4. 4Medication information — a current prescription or list of regular medication.

Medical records

Check what your authority requires.

TfL PCO: bring your full GP medical records dated within the last three months. NHS App access on its own is not enough.

Authorities requiring a medical summary: request a recent summary from your GP surgery. Check your council’s rules before attending.

How to request records

  1. Contact your GP surgery by phone, email or online portal.
  2. Give your full name, date of birth, address and NHS number if known.
  3. Ask for the full records or medical summary required by your licensing authority.
  4. Allow up to 28 days, then bring a digital or printed copy to your appointment.
