Medical records

Request the right records from your GP.

Your booking confirmation will show whether you need full GP medical records or a recent GP medical summary.

Bring a digital or printed copy. NHS App access alone is not enough unless your licensing authority specifically says it accepts it.

How to request records

  1. 1Contact your GP surgery by phone, email or online portal and request the records required for your driver medical.
  2. 2Give your full name, date of birth, address and NHS number if known.
  3. 3Ask for the full records or medical summary required by your licensing authority.
  4. 4Allow up to 28 days, then bring a digital or printed copy to your appointment.
