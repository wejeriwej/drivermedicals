Medical records

Request the right records from your GP.

Your council and vehicle licence decide whether you need full GP medical records or a recent GP medical summary. Your booking confirmation will tell you which applies.

Bring a digital or printed copy. NHS App access alone is not enough unless your licensing authority specifically says it accepts it.

How to request records

  1. Contact your GP surgery by phone, email or online portal and ask for the records required for your taxi or private-hire medical.
  2. Give your full name, date of birth, address and NHS number if known.
  3. Ask for the full records or medical summary required by your licensing authority.
  4. Allow up to 28 days, then bring a digital or printed copy to your appointment.
