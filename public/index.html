Medical records guide

How to request your medical records.

Some licensing authorities ask for full GP records before the medical can be completed. We recommend making your request early.

Request our free template →
  1. 1 Contact your GP surgery and request the records required by your authority — full records from birth where specified.
  2. 2 Provide your name, date of birth, address and NHS number if known.
  3. 3 Allow up to 28 days for your GP surgery to process the request.
  4. 4 Attend with a digital or printed copy of your records.

Helpful answers

Questions before your appointment?

Is the eye test included?

Yes. Your £43 session includes an eyesight check. Please bring glasses or contact lenses if you use them.

Do I need medical records?

It depends on your licensing authority. TfL PCO applicants should bring full GP medical records dated within the past three months; NHS App access alone is not sufficient.

Can I book for another council?

Call us on 07480 609640 first. We will confirm whether we can complete your authority’s form and what documents they require.
